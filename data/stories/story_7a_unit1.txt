 Finding a Friend at the Library
It was Saturday, and Tom was in the city library. The reading room was full of students, so he sat at the same table as a boy named Eric. Eric was reading a sports magazine, while Tom was finishing his Maths homework. Soon they started talking. Eric was very friendly and full of energy. He told Tom that his hobby was playing football and that he also liked writing for the school newspaper. Although they had different interests, they found it great fun to chat with each other. Before leaving, they decided to meet again the next weekend.


Two Sisters, One Great Friendship
Lucy and Lily are twin sisters. They study in the same class at the same school. Many people think they look the same, yet they have quite different hobbies. Lucy enjoys drawing and dreams of being an artist. Lily is fond of writing stories for the school newspaper. They often learn from each other: Lucy teaches Lily drawing while Lily helps Lucy improve her English. Both of them hope their dreams will come true some day.

Meet My New Classmate, Peter
Peter is my new classmate in Grade 7. Although we have different hobbies, we are good friends. Peter is full of energy every day. His favourite subject is science, and he wants to be a scientist in the future. In his free time, he likes reading science magazines. At the weekend, we often play basketball together after finishing our homework. It is always great fun to spend time with him!
