My Hobbies and Dream 
My name is Leo. I am a student in Grade 7. I have all kinds of hobbies. Every morning, I go jogging in the park to keep fit. At school, I like science best. I like to take old things apart and fix them. My friends think I am a smart boy.  I have a big dream. I want to be a scientist in the future. I hope my dream can come true one day!


Joining the Art Club
Amy loves painting and paper-cutting. She can paint beautiful flowers and animals. She wants to improve her skills and make new friends at school. So she decides to join the Art Club.  The club members have a meeting once a week. They show their works to each other. Amy’s role model is a famous artist. She would like to become a great designer. That sounds wonderful!

Our Club Life
Our school life is full of fun. There are many clubs in our school. My friend Daniel joins the Geography Club. He likes to learn about places around the world.
The Volleyball Club is also great. Each student works hard to learn the skill. In the future, we plan to visit a history museum together. We hope to learn new things and change our lives.
