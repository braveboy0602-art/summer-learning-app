Our Amazing Animal Friends 
Animals are our great friends, and they make our world colorful. Many animals have special abilities. For example, birds can fly high in the sky, and fish can swim freely in the water. Some animals, like dogs, are very clever and can even save people in danger.
However, many wild animals are in danger today because they are losing their homes. We should protect them and live in harmony with nature. If everyone gives a helping hand, our world will become a better place for both humans and animals.


A Trip to the Wildlife Park
Last Sunday, our class visited a big wildlife park. It was a sunny day, and we were all very excited. In the park, we saw many different animals, such as strong tigers, tall giraffes, and cute pandas.
The guide told us many interesting facts about these animals. We learned how they hunt for food and communicate with each other. We also took many photos and wrote down some notes. This trip was not only fun but also meaningful, because it taught us the importance of protecting nature and caring for animals.

Joining the Nature Club 
In our school, the Nature Club is very popular among students. The club members get together every Friday afternoon to learn about plants and animals. Sometimes, we walk around the campus to watch different birds and collect beautiful leaves.
Next month, the club plans to hold a photo show about local wildlife. We want to show our works to the whole school and tell everyone how to protect nature in daily life. Joining this club helps us learn new knowledge and make many good friends!
