 A Wonderful Trip to Beijing 
Traveling is a great way to open our eyes and learn about different cultures. Last summer, my family visited Beijing by train. The weather was bright and sunny, and everyone was excited.
We visited many famous places, such as the Palace Museum and the Great Wall. Walking on the ancient stones, we felt the long history of China. In the evening, we ate traditional roast duck, which was very delicious. During the trip, we took many beautiful photos together. It was an unforgettable experience, and I hope to visit more places in the future!


My Favorite Outdoor Hobby
Different people have different hobbies, and my favorite hobby is hiking in the mountains. Every month, my parents and I get together with some friends to go outdoors. Hiking helps us exercise our bodies and relax our minds after a busy week.
Along the way, we listen to the birds singing and enjoy the fresh air. Sometimes, we bring some bread and water to have a picnic on the top of the hill. Being close to nature makes us feel peaceful and happy. Healthy hobbies like this really make our lives full of energy!

Planning a Holiday Trip 
The winter holiday is coming, and our family is planning a trip to Hainan. My parents and I sat together yesterday to talk about our travel plan. We searched for information on the Internet and found many interesting activities.
We plan to stay near the sea for five days. We want to walk on the sandy beach, swim freely in the warm water, and try fresh seafood. Planning a trip together is as fun as the trip itself. We are all looking forward to this exciting holiday!
