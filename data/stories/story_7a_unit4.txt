Our Friendly Neighborhood 
I live in a warm and quiet neighborhood. People here are very friendly and always help each other. In the center of our neighborhood, there is a big noticeboard. We can learn about various important news and group activities on it.
Every weekend, many volunteers get together to clean up the park. They pick up rubbish and water the green trees. In the afternoon, kids play games while elders sit together and talk about their lives. I love my neighborhood because it feels like a big family, and everyone works hard to make it better!


A Meaningful Volunteer Day
Last Saturday was our community Volunteer Day. Early in the morning, our teacher showed us a notice on the school noticeboard. A small river near our school was very dirty, so we decided to form groups and do something for nature.
We brought bags and gloves to the river. Some students collected plastic bottles, and others cleaned the small paths. After three hours of hard work, the river became clean and beautiful again. We were tired, but we felt very proud. It is important to protect our home, and even small actions can make a big difference!

Helping Our Community
Every month, our class plans a special project to help our community. This time, we visited an old people’s home. Before we went, we posted a plan on the class noticeboard to collect books and flowers.
When we arrived, the old people were very happy to see us. We sang songs, read stories, and shared delicious food with them. Some students also helped them clean up their rooms. Before leaving, we took a photo together in the garden. This experience taught us that helping others brings real happiness to our lives.
