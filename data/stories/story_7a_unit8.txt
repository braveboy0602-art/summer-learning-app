Special Memories of the Year
Time flies quickly, and the school year is coming to an end. Looking back at the past few months, we have so many unforgettable memories. We still remember our first sports day, the class singing competition, and the fun trips with our friends.
During this year, we worked hard together, helped each other through difficult times, and shared many happy moments. Our teachers gave us continuous support and guidance. All these sweet experiences made our school lives rich and colorful. We will treasure these memories forever!


Preparing for the Class Party
To celebrate the end of the term, our class decided to hold a big party this Friday. Early in the morning, our teacher posted a plan on the noticeboard. Everyone was excited and wanted to make the party special.
We formed groups to prepare different things. Some students bought fruits and delicious snacks, while others decorated the classroom with colorful balloons. A few talented classmates practiced singing and dancing for the show. Working together for the party brought us closer, and we cannot wait for the happy Friday to arrive!

Looking Forward to the Future 
As we grow older, we begin to think more about our future. Secondary school opens a new chapter in our lives. We will learn more subjects, meet new friends, and face new challenges.
Although learning might become harder, we should stay confident and brave. In the coming years, we hope to discover new hobbies, build strong friendships, and become better versions of ourselves. With dreams in our hearts and hard work in our daily actions, we can surely create a bright future!
