Celebrating the Mid-Autumn Festival
The Mid-Autumn Festival is a traditional festival in China. It usually comes in autumn. On this day, relatives get together to celebrate the special time. In the evening, we sit in the yard and look at the round moon.
My mother makes delicious mooncakes for us. We eat them, drink tea, and talk about our lives. Everyone is full of joy. I really enjoy this warm night with my family!


The Harvest Season
Autumn is a great season because it is the time for harvest. Farmers work hard in the fields, but they have big smiles on their faces. They get a lot of fresh fruits and vegetables.
During this season, people also celebrate the harvest with their friends and relatives. They make delicious food and share their stories. Autumn is not cold or hot. It is a wonderful time to enjoy nature and feel the beauty of life.

 A Happy Family Reunion
Every year, my family has a big reunion during the holiday. My relatives come from different cities to visit us. We get together in my grandparents’ home.
We work together to clean the house and prepare a big dinner. My grandfather tells us traditional stories about our family. In the evening, we eat delicious dishes and play fun games. It is always a great time, and I hope we can meet more often in the future!
